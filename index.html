Personal Google Workspace Tools

This site is the registered homepage for a personal, single-user OAuth 2.0 application (the gog command-line client) owned and operated by Fabrizio Hernandez.

The application requests access only to the Google account of its owner (Gmail, Calendar, Drive, Docs, Sheets and Contacts) so that the owner can read and manage his own data from his own machines. It is not offered to the public, collects nothing from visitors, and has no other users.
