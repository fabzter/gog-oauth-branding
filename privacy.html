Privacy Policy

Personal Google Workspace Tools (the “gog” CLI) — last updated 2026-09-28

Who we are

This application is a personal, single-user command-line tool owned by Fabrizio Hernandez (“the owner”). It is not a public service and has no users other than the owner.

What data the application accesses

Using Google OAuth 2.0, the application accesses only the owner’s own Google account data: Gmail messages, Calendar events, Drive files, Docs, Sheets and Contacts. These scopes are used solely to let the owner read and manage his own data from his own devices.

Where data is stored

All OAuth tokens are stored locally in the operating-system keychain (macOS Keychain) on the owner’s machines. No Google data, and no tokens, are transmitted to, stored on, or processed by any third-party server. There is no backend.

What this website collects

This website is static. It sets no cookies, runs no analytics or trackers, and collects no personal information from visitors.

Sharing & retention

No data is shared, sold, or transferred to any third party. The owner may revoke the application’s access at any time from his Google Account permissions page, which immediately invalidates all stored tokens.
